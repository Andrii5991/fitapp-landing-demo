# Product messaging workbook & landing quiz

Use this document to **capture decisions about your app** in one place, then turn answers into **headlines, bullets, FAQ, and legal copy** for the site. Work top to bottom: answer the quiz, then copy the **Messaging snapshot** at the end into Notion, Linear, or your `i18n` source.

**Related:** see `docs/LANDING_SPEC.md` for page structure and technical notes for this repo.

---

## Part A — What a strong landing page must communicate

| Area | What visitors need | Your site should… |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Clarity** | What the product is in seconds | Name + category + one outcome (“workout tracker that shows progress”) |
| **Audience** | Whether it is for them | Say who it is for (e.g. gym-goers, yoga at home, coaches) |
| **Proof** | That it is real | Real screenshots, short video, store presence, or honest “beta” |
| **Action** | What to do next | One primary CTA (install / waitlist); secondary optional |
| **Trust** | That you are legitimate | Ratings, user scale, press, or founder credibility—only if true |
| **Platforms** | Where it runs | iOS, Android, web, watch—only what you ship |
| **Risk** | Privacy & terms | Links when you collect data, accounts, or analytics |

**Strongly recommended:** how it works (3–5 steps), 3–5 concrete benefits, FAQ (pricing, offline, data, account deletion), explicit pricing or “free / beta / coming soon.”

**Avoid:** vague superlatives, fake metrics, long text before any visual, missing privacy policy when you track or sign up users.
